Who I am

About me

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at UTAD, with one course remaining. Most of what I build sits between a database and a screen — geospatial backends, distributed sensor networks, mobile apps.

My final-year project, SmartBikeRoutes, was a two-person cycling app that routes over the real OpenStreetMap graph. Instead of ranking roads by distance, we wrote a cost function weighing gradient, weather and rider profile, so the route changes with the conditions instead of just warning you about them. It has produced 109 routes and 435 km so far.

Right now I'm building PitWall, an F1 strategy simulator that rebuilds real races from telemetry and tests the pit stop decisions that weren't made. It's also how I'm learning DevOps: the whole stack runs in Docker, CI runs on every push, and deployment is the step I'm on.

I've been working part-time in sales at Endesa alongside the degree. It's not engineering, but it did teach me to explain technical things to people who don't want a technical answer.
